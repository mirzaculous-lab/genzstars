Who we are

Gen Z Stars is a vibrant and dynamic communication society with six wings that work together to foster creativity, innovation and effective communication among our members.

With over 20 dedicated members, we are a diverse group who share a passion for communication and creativity. We are guided by our mentor, Doctor Muhammad Kashif, whose expertise and support shaped the society into what it is today.

Our six wings

Media MastersCamera, content and coverage
Literary ClubWords, writing and poetry
Stage StarsHosting, acting and performance
Tune StarsSinging and music
Debating WingArguments, rebuttals, confidence
Artistic EclipseDesign, art and visuals

Our purpose

We hunt and nurture talent within our community. We give members a platform to express themselves, sharpen their skills and become confident communicators. Whenever you need a place to shine, we are just a call away.

Our vision

To be the university's brightest home for creative voices: a society where every student finds a stage, builds the confidence to use it, and leaves as a communicator who stands out.
