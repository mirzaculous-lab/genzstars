Who We Are

Gen Z Stars is a dynamic communication society comprising six specialized wings. We are a diverse group of over 20+ passionate individuals dedicated to fostering creativity, innovation, and effective communication across the university.

Mentor's Vision

Guided by the visionary leadership of our mentor, Doctor Muhammad Kashif. His ongoing expertise and active support have been instrumental in shaping our society into what it is today.

Our Six Core Wings

Working in harmony to foster creativity, innovation, and effective communication.

1. Media Masters

3. Stage Stars

4. Tune Stars
